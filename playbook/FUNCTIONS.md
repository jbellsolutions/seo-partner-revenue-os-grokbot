# What each function means for a local SEO firm that grows through partners

The owner runs a local SEO firm: Google search and Google Maps, for US businesses. The growth engine is referral partners: agencies and freelancers whose clients ask "can you get us on Google?" The partner sends the client, the owner's team does the work, and the partner earns the commission in owner.md every month the client stays. Nobody is promised a ranking or an income.

## dream-100
The hundred partners where one yes brings a steady flow: web design and development agencies with many local-business clients, well-known WordPress and Wix builders, branding studios, marketing agencies that do PPC or social but not SEO, and the people who lead communities of them. People the owner named or met come first. The ask is a short call to see if a referral swap fits.

## dream-500
Owners of US agencies with 1 to 50 people in web design and development, WordPress and Wix, graphic design and branding, print and sign shops, PR and publicists, social media, PPC and email marketing, and hosting providers that serve local businesses. Ten at a time, each with one true first line about their shop. Never SEO agencies, lead-gen resellers, national franchise marketing firms, Shopify and e-commerce shops, chambers or BNI groups.

## partners
People who already have agency owners' attention: website-builder and hosting community managers, agency-owner groups and newsletters, accountants and coaches to agencies, white-label marketplaces. The ask: one introduction, a joint session, or a listing. What they get is the owner's to decide.

## cold-email
The agency owners no other agent is working, from public directories and the owner's own lists. The first message is one or two lines and asks one question: are they open to being referral partners. No links, no credentials and no tracking in a first message. The drafts export as a CSV for the sending tool (Instantly). The credibility comes after a reply, from Sales.

## lead-radar
Agency owners and freelancers asking in public about SEO for their clients: "client wants SEO", "white label SEO", "who do you use for local SEO", "SEO partner for my agency", "outsource SEO", "client asking about Google Maps", "Google Business Profile help". Where: r/web_design, r/WordPress, r/agency, r/freelance, r/smallbusiness, agency-owner Facebook groups and the communities in owner.md. A kept one is helped first, about what they asked.

## content
What the owner knows about local search that an agency can pass to its clients: Google Business Profiles, reviews, Maps rankings, what a website needs to be found locally, and how agencies earn from SEO without doing it. One post and one short video on the days it runs, in the owner's voice.

## speaker
Podcasts, groups and events for web designers, agency owners and small-business marketers. The angle is always something an agency can use with a client on Monday.

## aeo-seo
The questions an agency asks before picking an SEO partner: "white label local SEO", "SEO referral program for web designers", "best local SEO partner for agencies", "how to offer SEO without doing it", "Google Maps SEO for clients". One honest answer page each time, pointing to the partner page in owner.md.

## sales-crm
Answers every reply within minutes on weekdays (the Replies routine runs every ten minutes in business hours): the first answer to a cold-email or LinkedIn reply is the credibility message in owner.md, in the same thread, then two real times from the owner's calendar inside the owner's hours, and the call is booked once they pick one. After the call: one next step, the partner's details in the people list, and a thank-you when their first client signs. The people list is the CRM. One system answers replies: if the owner already has other agents answering, this one stays off until the owner picks.
