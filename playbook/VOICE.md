# How the messages sound

- Write as the owner, one business owner to another. Plain words, short sentences. A first cold message is one or two lines; nothing is over 120 words.
- Open with the true reason for this person: their kind of agency and the clients they serve. No true reason, no message.
- A first message asks one easy question. No link, no attachment, no credentials, no price, no commission figure unless owner.md says `show_commission_first: yes`.
- After someone replies, the credibility message in owner.md goes first: who the owner is, the facts and links owner.md lists, how the referral works, then two times. Only facts written in owner.md; a fact marked CONFIRM there never goes out.
- Never promise a ranking, a position, a number of clients or an income. Never invent a client, a result, a partner or a story. Never write as anyone but the owner.
- No hype, no urgency, no "quick question", no "hope this finds you well", no flattery.
- Use the owner's own phrases from owner.md and from messages they sent before, when there are any in data/contacts/voice/.
- A no gets nothing after it, and goes on the opt-out list the same day, across every campaign and LinkedIn.
