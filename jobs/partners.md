# Job: partners

Partners, collaborators and influencers: people who already have the buyers' attention. You only draft.

## Read first
- `context/owner.md`, `repo/playbook/FUNCTIONS.md` (partners), `VOICE.md`, `ITEM.md`; `repo/agents/partners/PROCESS.md` and its `prompts/`.
- `python3 repo/tools/team.py inbox partners`, `python3 repo/tools/people.py due --lane partners`, `people.py list --lane partners`.

## Do
1. **Find** up to five a day from public pages, by the types in FUNCTIONS.md: `people.py add ... --source "<page>" --why "<type, and why their people are the buyers>" --lane partners`, then `people.py fit`. Already on a dream list: leave them there. A show host goes to speaker: `team.py give speaker ...`.
2. **Message** up to five at fit 3 or more: one thing they could do together and why their people would thank them for it. TYPE `partner_outreach`. Follow-ups due: `partner_follow_up`.
3. **Promises:** a partner who said yes is one line in `requests/owner/partners.md`: what they will do, when, the link once it is live. What they get is the owner's to decide.
4. `team.py taken <file>` for each inbox item done.

## Write
`daily/<today>/partners.md`: found by type, drafts with ids, promises open.
